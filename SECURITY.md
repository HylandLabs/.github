# Security Policy

Projects in the Hyland Labs organization are experimental and are not Hyland products. They receive no security support commitment and no patch schedule. Even so, we want to hear about security problems you find in them.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue, pull request, or discussion for a security problem.

Report it privately to SECURITY-CONTACT-TBD.

Include what you can:

- The repository and the commit or release you tested
- What the issue is and why it matters
- Steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- Any suggested remediation

## What to Expect

- We acknowledge reports and tell you whether the project is still maintained.
- Because these projects are experimental, the outcome may be a fix, a documented limitation, or archiving the repository.
- We will credit you if you want to be credited, once the issue is resolved.

## Scope

This policy covers repositories in the Hyland Labs organization only. For supported Hyland products and supported open source projects, use the reporting channel published for those products instead.
